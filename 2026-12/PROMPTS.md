# Image prompts: December 2026

25 images: one banner per campaign, plus a realistic photo of each featured product in the campaign's setting. The FR and NL versions of each email use the same images, because the images contain no text.

## How to deliver

- **Folder:** save every image in this folder (`c:\Email marketing\images\2026-12\`) under the exact file name given.
- **Banners:** landscape (3:2), at least 1500 px wide. I crop them to a 2:1 strip, so keep the subject away from the edges.
- **Product photos:** square (1:1), at least 1000 x 1000 px.
- **Format:** JPG or PNG.
- **Deadline:** each campaign's images are due by its "deliver by" date. I then build the email and send you a test the day before it goes out.

## Realism block (add to the end of every prompt)

> Photorealistic photograph taken with a professional full-frame camera and a 35 mm or 50 mm lens, natural light, real location, authentic textures and small natural imperfections. It must not look like a 3D render, a mock-up or a stock template. Brand colours deep navy blue (#1F3D81) and warm orange (#EA5422) appear naturally in the printed items and a few accents. Printed surfaces show only abstract shapes or out-of-focus designs. No readable text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere, no watermarks. Hands are welcome; any people in the background are out of focus with faces not visible.

## Check before saving

Regenerate any image that shows readable text, a logo, warped hands or a product that looks fake.
